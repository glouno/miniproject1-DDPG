At every grid cell the first action is forced once; the rest of each rollout follows that checkpoint’s frozen actor without exploration. The physical state and five future-randomness seeds are shared. MC maxima are descriptive maxima of five sampled returns. This is one selected state; continuation policies change across checkpoints and earlier policies may never visit it.

No interpolation between checkpoints. Checkpoint playback gives every saved checkpoint equal screen time; the history axis shows actual training steps. Heatmaps use discrete measured cells. Blue Δ is below the actor baseline; red Δ is above it. Fixed colour ranges support comparison; fitting each checkpoint reveals smaller local differences. Grey paths show the shared reference prefix; the diamond marks the intervention, and crosses mark rollout endpoints.

The raw capture files preserve every observation, action and reward. Displayed paths and Q/G curves use up to 160 sampled states per rollout, plus the final endpoint; rollout playback advances through those samples. Censored rollouts are labelled and their G is the observed finite sum, without a critic-filled tail. Q/G curves include the full diagnostic continuation, including any steps beyond the 1,000-step performance boundary.
